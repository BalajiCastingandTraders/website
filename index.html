About Us

Crafting Components Since 2016

Established in 2016, Balaji Casting and Traders is a trusted name in non-ferrous casting, specializing in aluminum, copper, brass, and bronze alloys. We serve industries like printing, pipe manufacturing, rolling mills, and crane operations.

Our commitment to precision, innovation, and structured processes ensures transparency, reliability, and top-quality castings. At Balaji Casting and Traders, we prioritize excellence to exceed customer expectations.
